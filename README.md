# Steelwake: Survivors

A top-down naval roguelite inspired by Vampire Survivors and World of Warships.

## Concept

The player controls a warship and survives increasingly difficult waves of enemy ships.

The game combines:

- Manual ship movement
- Manual weapon aiming
- HE / AP / Torpedoes
- Armor and angling
- Ship classes
- Roguelite upgrades
- Enemy waves
- Boss encounters

## Ship Classes

### Destroyer
- Fast
- Low health
- Low armor
- Smoke
- Powerful torpedoes

### Cruiser
- Medium speed
- Medium health
- Medium armor
- Torpedo radar
- Balanced weapons

### Battleship
- Slow
- High health
- High armor
- Repair
- Reload boost
- Heavy guns
