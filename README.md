# Steelwake: Survivors

## Game Overview

**Steelwake: Survivors** is a top-down naval survival game inspired by *Vampire Survivors* and *World of Warships*. Players control a warship, fight waves of enemy vessels, collect experience, and choose upgrades to improve their chances of survival.

The game combines naval movement and aiming with roguelite progression, ship-specific abilities, and increasingly difficult enemy encounters.

## Core Features

* Top-down naval combat
* Ship movement, turning, acceleration, and reversing
* Mouse-aimed weapons
* High-explosive (HE) and armor-piercing (AP) ammunition
* Torpedo combat
* Armor and angling mechanics
* Multiple ship classes with distinct strengths and weaknesses
* Enemy waves and boss encounters
* Experience pickups and leveling
* Randomized upgrade choices
* Ship-specific abilities
* Health HUD, experience bar, game timer, and Game Over screen
* Restart functionality

## Ship Classes

### Destroyer — Playable

* High speed and maneuverability
* Low health and armor
* Smoke ability
* Torpedo-focused combat

### Cruiser — Playable

* Balanced speed, health, and armor
* Balanced main battery and torpedo capabilities
* Torpedo radar ability

### Battleship — Playable

* Low speed and maneuverability
* High health and armor
* Heavy main battery
* Repair ability
* Reload boost

### Frigate — Enemy

* Currently used as a non-playable enemy ship
* Intended to provide a lower-threat opponent
* Can appear alongside stronger enemy classes as difficulty increases
* Potential candidate for the first playable ship in a future version
